Privacy Policy — DeckOne

Effective date: 24 October 2026 (Asia/Hong_Kong)

DeckOne (“the App”) is provided by Omniwin Limited (“Omniwin,” “we,” “us”), a company incorporated in Hong Kong.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you use DeckOne on iOS in its current Mode B form: a local-first one-task deck and focus timer, with optional in-app purchases through Apple.

It applies only to DeckOne. It does not cover other Omniwin products or websites unless those products link to their own policies.

If you do not agree with this Policy, do not use the App.

2. What DeckOne is (and is not)

DeckOne is a productivity utility. It is not a medical device, therapy, diagnosis tool, or treatment for any condition. Public descriptions of the App are utility-only.

The App is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children and is not offered in Apple’s Kids Category.

3. Information that stays on your device

By design, your deck cards, timer/session history, settings, and 18+ attestation are stored locally on your device (on-device database). Omniwin does not operate a DeckOne cloud account or cloud backup for this Mode B release.

Uninstalling the App, clearing App data, or using Delete everything on this device in Settings removes that local content from the device (subject to ordinary device backups you control, such as your own iCloud/device backups).

4. Information that may leave your device

4.1 Apple In-App Purchases and RevenueCat

If you buy or restore a subscription or other in-app purchase:

  • Apple processes the payment and related App Store account / receipt information under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.
  • We use RevenueCat to manage entitlements and restore purchases. RevenueCat may process purchase-related identifiers and entitlement status so the App can unlock paid features. See RevenueCat’s privacy documentation for its processing role.

Omniwin does not receive your full payment card number through DeckOne.

4.2 Support email

If you email us (including via in-App email to contact@theomniwin.com), we receive the content of that message, your email address, and any attachments you include, so we can respond. Keep medical or sensitive health details out of support mail unless you choose to send them; DeckOne is not a clinical service.

4.3 Export

If you use Export deck, the App creates a local file (or share payload) of your deck data that you choose where to send (Files, AirDrop, another app, etc.). Omniwin does not receive that export unless you deliberately send it to us.

4.4 What we do not collect in this Mode B build

On the current App Store binary this Policy describes, DeckOne does not include separate product-analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs (for example PostHog, Sentry, or Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics), does not use App Tracking Transparency / IDFA for cross-app tracking, and does not require a DeckOne login or cloud sync.

If we add analytics, crash reporting, accounts, or cloud sync later, we will update this Policy and App Store privacy labels before relying on that change in a public release.

5. How we use information

We use information only to:

  • provide and improve DeckOne’s local features;
  • process and restore in-app purchases / entitlements;
  • respond to support requests;
  • meet legal obligations and protect Omniwin Limited, users, and the App against abuse or fraud.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Sharing

We share information only as needed with:

  • Apple (App Store / IAP);
  • RevenueCat (entitlement / purchase tooling);
  • service providers who host our email or corporate systems, under appropriate arrangements;
  • authorities when required by law.

We do not share DeckOne user data with Samakasa, PremioKey, or other Omniwin product stacks for their marketing.

7. Retention

  • On-device data: retained until you delete it, clear App data, or uninstall (plus any device backups you control).
  • Support emails: retained as long as needed to handle your request and our ordinary business / legal records.
  • Purchase / entitlement records: retained as needed to provide the subscription, prevent fraud, and meet accounting/tax/App Store requirements (often via Apple and RevenueCat systems).

8. Your choices

  • Use the App offline for core deck/timer features (purchases and restore need network / App Store).
  • Export your deck from Settings.
  • Delete local App data from Settings (Delete everything on this device).
  • Manage or cancel Apple subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings (and via the App’s Plan / Manage path where available).
  • Contact contact@theomniwin.com for privacy questions about DeckOne.

Hong Kong users may have rights under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) regarding personal data we hold. Contact us to make a request. Some requests may be limited where we do not hold the data (for example, purely on-device content we never received).

9. International processing

Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase-related data in countries outside Hong Kong. Where we transfer personal data, we take steps appropriate to the context and applicable law.

10. Children’s privacy

DeckOne is for users 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through DeckOne. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us by email, contact contact@theomniwin.com and we will take appropriate steps.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. The Effective date at the top will change when we do. Material changes that affect how DeckOne handles data will be reflected in the App Store privacy labels and this URL before a release that relies on them.
